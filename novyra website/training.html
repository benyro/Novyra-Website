Training & tips

Short reads you can act on tonight: hooks, retention, energy, and the boring stuff that actually matters (lighting, sleep, audio).

Content strategy

One strong short-form idea per day beats five random posts. Batch film, reuse hooks, and point traffic to your LIVE window.

LIVE performance

Open with a promise (“Tonight we rank / we learn / we celebrate X”). Thank gifts by name. End with a clear comeback time.

Starter checklist

  • Key light in front, soft fill, no ceiling-only lighting
  • Phone elevated at eye line, because eye line sells trust
  • Stable username and pinned rules for chat
  • Two backup talking points if chat goes quiet
